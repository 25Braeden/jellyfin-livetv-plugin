This codebase has gone through thorough hardening across Gates 1–3.5. The core design—combining native BlockedTags with a default-deny IAsyncActionFilter, immutable AccessSnapshot references, deep reflection-based argument walking, and JSON copy-scrubbing—closes the vast majority of standard bypass vectors.

However, under the threat model of authenticated non-admin users and anonymous internet callers behind Caddy, there are 6 concrete vulnerabilities and edge cases in the bundle, ranked below from High to Low severity.
